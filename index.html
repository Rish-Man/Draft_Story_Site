Hey, you found Verv.

Quick heads-up: this isn't one of those sites with a thousand pop-ups, autoplay videos, and articles written by robots. It's just a place for good stories and good jokes, curated by an actual human — me.

Every piece you see here earned its spot. Some made me laugh out loud on a crowded train. Some made me sit quietly for a minute after reading. Some I read twice just to enjoy the way they were written. That's the bar.

If you write — or if you've ever thought "I could write better than this" — don't keep it to yourself.

Slide into my DMs on Instagram at @verv.reads. Send me your stories, your jokes, your half-finished drafts, your weird ideas. I genuinely love reading them, and I'm always down to give feedback, swap notes, or just chat about what makes writing work.

No gatekeeping here. Oh btw, I am also not a great writer. It's just something I do for passing time.
